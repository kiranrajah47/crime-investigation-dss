CASE ID: 2024-047
DATE OF INCIDENT: 8th April 2024
REPORTING OFFICER: Inspector Mohan Rao

VICTIM DETAILS:
Name        : Priya Sharma
Age         : 32
Occupation  : Senior Accountant, Meridian Finance Ltd.
Address     : Flat 4B, Sunshine Apartments, Kodialbail, Mangaluru

INCIDENT SUMMARY:
Priya Sharma was found unconscious near the rear entrance of the abandoned Kamath Warehouse
on MG Road, Mangaluru at approximately 11:15 PM on the night of 8th April 2024.
She was discovered by a passerby, Mr. Sunil D'Souza, who immediately called the police.

The victim was rushed to KMC Hospital where she was declared dead on arrival.
Preliminary medical examination suggests blunt force trauma to the back of the head
as the probable cause of death. Time of death is estimated between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM.

VICTIM BACKGROUND:
Priya Sharma had been working at Meridian Finance Ltd. for the past four years.
Colleagues describe her as professional and hardworking. In the weeks leading up to her death,
she had reportedly been under significant stress related to a large financial discrepancy
she discovered in company accounts.

She had confided in her close friend Anita Pais that she suspected someone at her workplace
was involved in embezzlement and that she had collected evidence. She had planned to report
this to the management the following week.

KNOWN CONFLICTS:
1. Priya had an unresolved financial dispute with her colleague Rajan Shetty over an
   unpaid personal loan of Rs. 50,000 given six months prior. Multiple colleagues witnessed
   heated arguments between them in the office over the past two months.

2. Priya had recently filed a formal HR complaint against her reporting manager Devika Nair
   for workplace harassment and unfair performance reviews.

3. A neighbour reported that a man matching the description of Rajan Shetty was seen
   waiting outside Priya's apartment building on the evening of the incident around 7:30 PM.

LAST KNOWN MOVEMENTS:
- 6:00 PM : Left office at Meridian Finance Ltd.
- 7:45 PM : Seen at a nearby tea stall by a witness
- 9:00 PM : Received a phone call (duration: 4 minutes) — caller identity under investigation
- 10:00 PM : Last confirmed sighting near MG Road by a shopkeeper
- 11:15 PM : Found unconscious near Kamath Warehouse
