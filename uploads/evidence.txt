CASE ID: 2024-047
EVIDENCE REPORT
Prepared by: Forensic Officer Rekha Shenoy
Date: 9th April 2024

PHYSICAL EVIDENCE RECOVERED AT SCENE:

1. WEAPON (PRIMARY):
   A heavy iron rod approximately 45 cm in length was recovered 8 metres from the victim.
   Traces of blood on the rod have been sent for DNA analysis. Partial fingerprint
   detected on the lower grip of the rod — forensic lab analysis pending.

2. BROKEN GLASS BOTTLE:
   Found near the victim's right hand. Contents identified as a local liquor brand.
   No useful fingerprints recovered from the bottle surface.

3. VICTIM'S BELONGINGS:
   - Handbag found at the scene with wallet intact — robbery ruled out as motive.
   - Mobile phone recovered. Last call received at 9:00 PM. Caller ID: saved as "R.S."
   - A folded paper found inside the handbag contained handwritten account figures
     and the note: "R.S. — Rs. 2.3 Cr diverted — proof saved in office locker."

4. BLUE JACKET BUTTON:
   A single button matching a formal blue jacket was found near the crime scene.
   The button has a distinctive silver emblem. Investigating officers noted that
   Rajan Shetty was seen wearing a blue jacket earlier that day per CCTV footage.

5. CCTV FOOTAGE:
   - Camera 1 (MG Road shop): Shows a man in a blue jacket walking toward the warehouse
     at 10:12 PM. Face not fully visible but build and jacket match Rajan Shetty.
   - Camera 2 (ATM near warehouse): Captures a woman matching victim's description
     entering the lane at 10:05 PM, followed by a male figure 3 minutes later.
   - Camera 3 (Sunshine Apartments): Shows Rajan Shetty's vehicle parked outside
     victim's apartment building at 7:28 PM for approximately 25 minutes.

WITNESS STATEMENTS:

W1 — Kavitha Amin (neighbour of victim):
   "I clearly saw Rajan sir arguing loudly with Priya outside her flat around 7:30 in
   the evening. He was pointing his finger aggressively. She looked very scared."

W2 — Sunil D'Souza (person who discovered victim):
   "I was walking past the warehouse around 11:15 PM when I saw the lady lying on the
   ground. I did not see anyone else nearby at that time. I called the police immediately."

W3 — Ramesh (tea stall owner near office):
   "Priya regularly comes here. That day she was with a woman I had not seen before —
   maybe around 50 years old, well dressed. They looked like they were having a serious
   conversation. This was around 7:45 PM."

W4 — Gopal (night security guard, Meridian Finance):
   "Devika madam came back to the office around 10:30 PM that night. She said she forgot
   her laptop. She was in the office for about 20 minutes. I logged her entry and exit."

PHONE RECORDS:
   - Victim received a 4-minute call at 9:00 PM from a number registered to Rajan Shetty.
   - Victim sent a WhatsApp message at 9:48 PM to contact 'Anita' reading: "Meeting someone
     near MG Road. If I don't reply by 11 just call the police. I have the proof."
   - Devika Nair's phone pinged a tower near MG Road at 10:20 PM — contradicts her
     claim of being at home that evening.

FORENSIC NOTES:
   - Soil on victim's shoes matches soil composition near the warehouse — confirms
     she was at the scene before her collapse.
   - No foreign DNA recovered from victim's clothing yet — lab results pending.
   - Time of death confirmed between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM based on body temperature.
