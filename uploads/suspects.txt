SUSPECT: Rajan Shetty

Age         : 35
Occupation  : Senior Accounts Executive, Meridian Finance Ltd.
Address     : 12, Palm Grove Layout, Bejai, Mangaluru
Relation    : Colleague of victim

BACKGROUND:
Rajan Shetty has been working at Meridian Finance Ltd. for six years.
He is the direct colleague of the victim Priya Sharma and both worked in the same
accounts department. Rajan had borrowed Rs. 50,000 from the victim eight months ago
as a personal loan and had not repaid the amount despite repeated requests.
Multiple colleagues confirm that Rajan and Priya had heated arguments over this
outstanding amount in the weeks before the incident.

CONFLICT WITH VICTIM:
Rajan had an ongoing financial dispute with the victim over the unpaid loan.
Witnesses at the office report that Rajan had told Priya to "stop following him"
and that he would "deal with it in his own way." Priya's friend Anita confirmed
that Priya was afraid of Rajan and had mentioned that he had been aggressive.

ALIBI:
Rajan claims he was at home alone on the night of the incident from 8:00 PM onward.
He states he spoke to the victim on phone at 9:00 PM to "sort out the loan issue."
No independent witness can confirm he was home after 8:00 PM.
His vehicle was spotted on CCTV outside the victim's apartment at 7:28 PM.
CCTV near the warehouse shows a man in a blue jacket matching his description at 10:12 PM.
His phone records confirm the 9:00 PM call to the victim — lasting 4 minutes.

PAST CRIMINAL RECORD:
One prior case of assault filed in 2019 against a former colleague.
Charges were later dropped after an out-of-court settlement.
A restraining order was filed against him by a former partner in 2021 — later withdrawn.
History of aggressive behaviour and conflict in previous workplace.

ADDITIONAL LINKS TO EVIDENCE:
The button found at the crime scene matches buttons on a blue jacket seen in Rajan's
social media photos. His initials R.S. match the contact name saved on the victim's phone.
The folded note found in the victim's bag references "R.S." in connection to embezzlement.
Forensic fingerprint analysis of the iron rod is pending — result may confirm or rule out.

SUSPECT: Devika Nair

Age         : 47
Occupation  : Senior Manager (Accounts), Meridian Finance Ltd.
Address     : 8, Officers Colony, Kadri, Mangaluru
Relation    : Reporting manager and supervisor of the victim

BACKGROUND:
Devika Nair has been with Meridian Finance for eleven years and is the direct reporting
manager of the victim. In the two months before the incident, Priya Sharma had filed
a formal HR complaint against Devika alleging workplace harassment, biased performance
reviews, and deliberate exclusion from key projects.

CONFLICT WITH VICTIM:
The HR complaint filed by the victim was a significant threat to Devika's career and
reputation. Sources within the company suggest Devika was aware that Priya had discovered
financial irregularities in company accounts and had compiled evidence pointing toward
senior management involvement. Devika stood to lose her position and face legal action
if the victim's findings were reported to management.

ALIBI:
Devika initially told investigators she was at home the entire evening of the incident.
However, her phone pinged a mobile tower near MG Road at 10:20 PM — directly contradicting
her claim. Security guard Gopal confirmed she returned to the Meridian Finance office at
10:30 PM claiming she forgot her laptop — unusually late for a weeknight.
No witness can confirm her whereabouts between 9:30 PM and 10:30 PM.
The unidentified well-dressed woman seen with Priya at 7:45 PM at the tea stall
matches Devika's general description per witness W3.

PAST RECORD:
No prior criminal record.
A previous employee at Meridian Finance filed an internal complaint against Devika in 2021
for manipulating performance records — the complaint was dismissed internally.
She has a reputation among junior staff for being controlling and retaliatory.

ADDITIONAL LINKS TO EVIDENCE:
Devika's phone location data places her near the crime scene at the estimated time of death.
Her late return to the office is uncharacteristic and unverified.
She had direct motive — the victim's planned disclosure could end her career.

SUSPECT: Suresh Kamath

Age         : 41
Occupation  : Warehouse Manager, Kamath Logistics (unrelated to crime scene warehouse)
Address     : 22, Balmatta Road, Mangaluru
Relation    : Distant acquaintance of victim — met through a common friend

BACKGROUND:
Suresh Kamath has a passing acquaintance with the victim through a mutual friend,
Anita Pais. He had met Priya at two social gatherings in the past year.
There is no known financial relationship, professional connection, or conflict
between Suresh Kamath and the victim.

CONFLICT WITH VICTIM:
No known conflict of any kind. No financial dispute. No professional rivalry.
No complaints or mentions of Suresh Kamath in any of the victim's personal
communications reviewed by investigators.

ALIBI:
Suresh Kamath has a strong and fully verified alibi for the night of the incident.
He attended a family wedding reception at Hotel Ocean Pearl, Mangaluru from 7:00 PM
to midnight. Over fifteen independent witnesses including family members, hotel staff,
and other guests confirm his presence throughout the evening.
CCTV from Hotel Ocean Pearl confirms Suresh Kamath was present at the venue at 10:15 PM —
the same time the crime is estimated to have occurred.
He is fully accounted for and not present at the scene.

PAST RECORD:
No prior criminal record of any kind.
No complaints, disputes, or legal matters on record.
Well regarded in his community and workplace.

CONCLUSION ON THIS SUSPECT:
Based on available evidence, alibi verification, and absence of motive or conflict,
Suresh Kamath is considered a low priority suspect. No further investigation
of this individual is recommended at this stage unless new evidence emerges.
